# Regional Seasons V2.1 — experimental source preview

Fabric 1.21.1. Four seasons, local cold snaps, 18-layer snow, regional coherent precipitation fields, a pixelated weather radar with a roughly 1,024-block radius, battery-powered blizzard radio, 7–10 minute fictional colds, temperature-based survival effects and a craftable passive AC fan. Forecast-calendar blur rendering is adjusted and the Fahrenheit HUD is rounded and stabilized. **This is not a verified release.** Compile and test before distributing. Local radar temperature shading represents approximate conditions, not scanned biome values; dry-biome boundary smoothing remains incomplete.
